Cookie policy

Here is what to know about browser cookies when using the current VOVEOFRICA application.

Draft for review

Review this notice against the production configuration and any third-party services before publishing. Cookie behavior can change when analytics, payments, or other integrations are added.

Essential session cookie

The application uses Flask's session mechanism to keep track of signed-in users and other session state. This cookie supports core account features and is not an advertising cookie.

Optional cookies and browser controls

No analytics or advertising cookie integration is configured in the current application code. If optional tracking is added, this policy and any required consent controls should be updated before it is enabled.

You can use your browser settings to inspect or clear cookies. Clearing the session cookie may sign you out or interrupt features that depend on your session.
