Frequently asked questions

Common questions

How do I get started?

Create an account, complete your profile, then visit Discover to explore connections. You can also review your matches and messages from the navigation.

How can I report a concern?

Use our contact options to tell us what happened. Include relevant details, but avoid sharing passwords or other sensitive account credentials.

Where can I see subscription prices?

Visit the plans page to see the free community tier, Premium one-time pass prices, and the optional 24-hour Boost in Nigerian naira. Purchases do not auto-renew.

When does paid access begin?

Premium access or a Boost begins only after Paystack payment confirmation. Visit Plan and purchases to see status and the payment reference. If a payment was taken but access is not active, contact support with that reference.

Is VOVEOFRICA responsible for in-person meetings?

We cannot guarantee another user’s identity, conduct, or offline safety. Take time to get to know someone, protect your personal information, and choose a public place if you decide to meet.
