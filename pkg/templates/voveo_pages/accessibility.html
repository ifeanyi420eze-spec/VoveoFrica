Accessibility statement

We want VOVEOFRICA to be usable by as many people as possible and welcome feedback that helps us improve.

Ongoing work

The application has not yet been independently audited against an accessibility standard. This page describes our intent and a way to report barriers; it is not a claim of full conformance.

Keep improving

We aim to improve page structure, readable content, keyboard access, and responsive layouts as the product develops.

Tell us what blocked you

When reporting an accessibility barrier, include the page, the task you were trying to complete, and any assistive technology details you are comfortable sharing.

Protect your information

Please do not include passwords, verification codes, or sensitive personal information in an accessibility request.
