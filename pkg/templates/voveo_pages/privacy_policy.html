A plain-language starting point for understanding information handled by the current VOVEOFRICA application.

Draft for review

Information used by the application

When you register, the application stores the account details requested by its form, including your name, username, email address, phone number, location, and a password hash. Profiles, likes, matches, reports, notifications, subscriptions, and chat messages are stored when you use those features.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card numbers, or other credentials in chat or support messages.

Why it is used

Account and profile information supports sign-in and the app's discovery and matching features. Message and report information supports the features you choose to use.

Account security

Passwords are stored as password hashes rather than readable passwords. Use a unique password and keep your sign-in details private.

Questions or requests

Contact us to ask about your information or request help with your account. The response process and retention periods should be confirmed before this notice is finalized.
